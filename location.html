Main Celebration Hub

The largest public celebration for Enkutatash 2017 will be held at Meskel Square in the heart of Addis Ababa. This is where the Chibo bonfire gathering and the main concert take place.

Address

Meskel Square, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia

Parking & Transport

Light Rail Transit (LRT) is recommended. Parking available at Exhibition Center.
