The Dawn of Enkutatash

The Ethiopian New Year, known as Enkutatash, falls on the 11th of September (or the 12th in leap years). It marks the end of the rainy season and the beginning of spring, a time when the Ethiopian highlands burst into a vibrant canvas of bright yellow Meskel flowers.

Enkutatash is more than just a change in the calendar; it is a time of renewal, forgiveness, and hope. Families gather to share traditional meals, sing festive songs, and light bonfires (Chibo) to symbolize the transition from darkness to light.
